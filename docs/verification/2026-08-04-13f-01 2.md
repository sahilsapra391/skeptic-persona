## The two [VERIFY] items, resolved from primary sources

**Whole-dollar values.** Form 13F FAQ (sec.gov, fetched 2026-08-04 with
declared UA): the 2022 amendments "Simplif[y] the rounding conventions of Form
13F to require that the dollar values reported be rounded to the nearest
dollar (rather than to the nearest one thousand dollars)" — effective
**2023-01-03** (adopted in Release 34-95148, 2022-06-23; FAQ sections carry
"Updated: January 3, 2023"). Our scope (2025+) is entirely whole-dollar.
**Live confirmation:** MERIDIAN's real Q2 filing reports AIRBNB 9,092 sh at
value 1,301,065 → implied ~$143/sh. Dollars, not thousands.

**Options rows.** FAQ Q44 (Updated: January 3, 2023), quoted: "Column 5 is
where you actually indicate that the listing is an option because **most of
the column entries for an option refer to the underlying security, rather
than to the option itself (i.e., Columns 1-5 and 7-8)**." So `value` and
`sshPrnamt` on a put/call row are the **underlying's** value and share count.
Encoded in the parser contract (form13f.ts docblock); 13F-04's templates must
carry the same convention (licensed shape: "puts covering N shares of X,
underlying value $Y as of <period>"). Also load-bearing for copy law: FAQ Q41
(no short positions) and Q43 (written options never reported) — "net exposure
can never be inferred" is the SEC's own structure, not our caution.

## Deviations from the plan, stated

1. **Metadata-lite for non-watchlist filers.** The plan's filings_13f row
   lists period + infotable size for all filers; both require a per-filing
   index fetch, which at ~5k deadline-day filings is thousands of fetches for
   rows the plan itself says not to pay to parse. Non-watchlist rows carry
   what the feed itself states (cik, name, form, filed_at, accession); period
   and size stay NULL. Enrichment happens only for watchlist CIKs. This is
   the plan's own "do not pay" principle applied one level earlier.
2. **The quarantine digest line** ("filing failed sanity checks") lands with
   13F-04's digest work — the quarantine mechanics (status, reason, no rows,
   loud log) are complete now; the digest sentence belongs to the deadline
   digest that 13F-04 builds. Owner's cut-order guidance puts digest work
   below sanity gates, which are fully in.
3. **The heavy-lane Actions parse** for `deferred_heavy` filings ships with
   13F-03's backfill job (same code path, one implementation). Deferred rows
   are visible by status and re-openable; nothing is silently dropped.
