## openFIGI, live-verified (acceptance: 20 real CUSIPs)

Two batches of 10 jobs (no API key) against `POST /v3/mapping`, using the
first 20 distinct CUSIPs of the real MERIDIAN filing: **19 mapped, 1
legitimate miss** (`N07059210`, a CINS — the fail-open case). Responses frozen
as fixtures.

**The selection rule came from the data, not the docs:**

- FIRST HORIZON (320517105): `data[0..4]` are FT2 on German venues; FHN/US
  sits at position 6. `data[0]` would put a **German ticker** on the account.
- EXXON (30231G102): eleven entries, **zero** with `exchCode US` (Kazakh,
  Uzbek, German venues). Any pick is a wrong ticker.

Rule shipped: `exchCode === "US"` wins; otherwise **no ticker** and the post
falls open to the issuer name the filing itself states. `$XOM_KZ` is a
fabrication; "EXXON MOBIL CORP" is licensed by the record.

Misses are cached (`openfigi_nodata`) so a CUSIP is asked once. A transport
failure or a **misaligned response caches nothing** — zipping wrong rows
would cache a wrong ticker against a CUSIP, which is a fabrication vector,
not a glitch.

Worker-egress note: api.openfigi.com verified from residential; the Worker's
own egress is confirmed by the first live resolution after deploy (visible as
cusip_map rows; a failure logs per tick and caches nothing).
